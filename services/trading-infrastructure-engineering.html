The real failure mode

Trading systems usually fail from boring infrastructure problems.

Many traders do not fail because of one bad strategy idea. They fail because the system around the idea is fragile.

  • IB Gateway disconnects and recovery is manual.
  • Data feeds stop after resets or subscriptions drift.
  • Logs do not explain what happened during failure.
  • Backtests are hard to reproduce or compare.
  • CSV, Parquet, and database workflows collect dirty bars.
  • Deployment depends on memory, SSH sessions, or one laptop.
