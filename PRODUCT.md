# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is trading firms and founders assessing whether Fisher Lok can design, improve, or build serious quantitative research and live-trading infrastructure.

Secondary audiences include technical readers and collaborators interested in Fisher's thinking about artificial intelligence and technology through the blog.

## Product Purpose

The website establishes Fisher as a precise, technically exceptional, and trustworthy quantitative engineer. Its primary business goal is to create qualified consulting conversations about trading infrastructure. Its secondary purpose is to publish and share Fisher's ideas about AI, technology, mathematics, markets, and engineering.

Success means that a relevant trading firm or founder can quickly understand Fisher's capabilities and decide to start a focused consulting conversation, while readers can discover and follow his long-form thinking.

## Positioning

Fisher works across the path from quantitative research to production-minded trading infrastructure. The website should demonstrate both technical depth and the ability to turn research workflows into reliable systems, supported by real projects, services, experience, and writing already present in the repository.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate Fisher by reviewing featured projects, trading-infrastructure services, professional experience, the résumé, and long-form blog posts. The primary path ends in direct contact by email. The site is an existing static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript website that must work across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the website's existing factual content, project links, blog posts, résumé, service information, and contact destination unless Fisher explicitly approves copy changes.
- Keep consulting for quantitative and trading infrastructure as the primary conversion goal.
- Keep the blog as a meaningful secondary surface for AI and technology thinking rather than treating it as filler beneath the portfolio.
- Do not invent performance figures, clients, testimonials, trading returns, deployment claims, or commercial outcomes.
- The current Apple-inspired demo is an anti-reference for the redesign: future work must express Fisher's identity rather than borrowing another company's visual identity.

## Brand Commitments

- Dominant identity: precise, technically exceptional, trustworthy quantitative engineer.
- Primary audience: trading firms and founders.
- Primary outcome: consulting enquiries about trading and quantitative infrastructure.
- Secondary identity: thoughtful researcher-builder who shares ideas about AI and technology.
- The Fisher Lok name, existing work, and existing factual claims remain authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

- Homepage project descriptions and links in `index.html`.
- Trading infrastructure consulting details in `services/trading-infrastructure-engineering.html`.
- Professional experience and skills in `resume/index.html`.
- Long-form writing in `blog/` covering AI, technology, mathematics, markets, music, and personal trading experience.
- Existing project imagery in `assets/images/`.
- No verified client testimonials, trading-performance claims, or consulting case-study outcomes are currently documented; future design work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with credible evidence of engineering judgment, not borrowed prestige or generic technical decoration.
2. Help trading firms understand Fisher's consulting relevance quickly and confidently.
3. Make the path from research ideas to reliable trading systems legible.
4. Treat technical writing as a serious expression of Fisher's thinking and a secondary reason to return.
5. Preserve factual accuracy and distinguish demonstrated work from unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website should remain understandable and navigable with keyboard input, assistive technology, reduced motion, and mobile or zoomed layouts. Important destinations such as services, experience, writing, and contact must not disappear on smaller screens.
